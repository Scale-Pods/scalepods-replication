## 11. SEO Canonical Compliance Rules & Best Practices

1. **Rule of 100% 200 OK Sitemaps**:  
   Every URL listed inside `https://www.scalepods.co/sitemap.xml` MUST return HTTP 200 OK and MUST have a self-referential canonical tag. No redirecting (301/302/307) or canonicalized-away URL should ever be in the sitemap.
2. **Case Study Canonical Protocol**:  
   Always link directly to `https://www.scalepods.co/case-study/[slug]` across sitewide navigation, internal articles, and external distribution.
3. **Internal Linking Integrity**:  
   All links in the sitewide Footer (`Footer.tsx`), homepage sections, and navigation bars must point strictly to final canonical URLs (e.g. `/solution/ops-workflow-automation` rather than `/solution/operations-automation`).
4. **Google Search Console Indexing**:  
   When adding new case studies or articles:
   - Verify they appear in `sitemap.xml`.
   - Submit the URL in Google Search Console URL Inspection.
   - Click "Test Live URL" to ensure Googlebot fetches the page with HTTP 200 and matches the declared canonical tag.
